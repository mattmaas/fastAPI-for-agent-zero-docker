### Summary of Sources on the "Son of Man" in Daniel and Revelation

#### 1. **Who is the One like a Son of Man in Daniel 7:13? (GotQuestions.org)**
- **Context**: Daniel's vision in chapter 7 includes four beasts representing kingdoms, and the "One like a Son of Man" who comes before the Ancient of Days (God).
- **Details of the Vision**:
  - **Beasts**: 
    - First: Lion with eagle's wings (Daniel 7:4).
    - Second: Bear raised on one side with three ribs (Daniel 7:5).
    - Third: Leopard with four wings and four heads (Daniel 7:6).
    - Fourth: Terrifying beast with iron teeth and ten horns (Daniel 7:7-8).
  - **Judgment Scene**: The Ancient of Days takes His throne, and judgment is pronounced against the beasts (Daniel 7:9-12).
  - **Son of Man**: 
    - Comes with the clouds of heaven (Daniel 7:13).
    - Given dominion, glory, and an everlasting kingdom (Daniel 7:14).
- **Interpretation**: The "Son of Man" is often identified with Jesus, who referred to Himself by this title, indicating His messianic role and authority.

#### 2. **Daniel’s Vision of the Son of Man (Ligonier Ministries)**
- **Vision Overview**: Daniel's vision occurs in the first year of Belshazzar (Daniel 7:1).
- **Beasts and Judgment**: The four beasts symbolize four kingdoms, and the vision culminates in a divine judgment scene where the "Son of Man" is presented to the Ancient of Days.
- **Significance of the Son of Man**: 
  - He is given an everlasting dominion, contrasting with the temporary nature of the beasts' kingdoms.
  - The interpretation of the vision emphasizes the establishment of God's eternal kingdom.

#### 3. **Seeing the Son of Man: How Reading Daniel and Revelation Together Illuminates Both (Via Emmaus)**
- **Intertextual Connections**: The imagery of the "Son of Man" in Daniel is echoed in Revelation, particularly in the depiction of Christ.
- **Revelation's Use of Daniel**: John’s vision in Revelation 1:13-16 draws heavily from Daniel's descriptions, portraying Jesus as a kingly and priestly figure.
- **Symbolism**: The "Son of Man" in Revelation embodies both divine authority and the fulfillment of God's promises.

#### 4. **Who is the Son of Man in Revelation 14:14? (Reading Acts)**
- **Context**: Revelation 14:14 depicts a figure "like a son of man" sitting on a cloud, holding a sickle.
- **Interpretation**: 
  - This figure is debated as either the Messiah or an angel.
  - The imagery connects back to Daniel 7, suggesting a role in divine judgment.
- **Symbolism of the Cloud**: The cloud signifies divine authority and glory, aligning with the portrayal of Jesus in the Gospels.
